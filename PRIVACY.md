# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** March 31, 2026

Discord Account Manager is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension is designed as an open-source tool and operates with a "local-first" principle.

### 1. Data Collection and Usage

- **Authentication Data:** The extension stores your Discord tokens to allow you to switch between accounts.
- **Account Metadata:** Usernames and profile pictures (avatars) are stored to help you identify your accounts.
- **Local Storage:** All data is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`). We **do not** collect, store, or transmit your data to any external servers.

### 2. Third-Party Sharing

- We **do not** share, sell, or trade your personal information or authentication tokens with any third parties.

### 3. Security

- Your tokens are as sensitive as your passwords. This extension is open-source, allowing anyone to verify that your data remains only on your device.
